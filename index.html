Methodology

Inputs and preprocessing. Historical fields are taken from ERA5 and joined to the selected ECMWF seasonal initialization. OLR is derived from top-of-atmosphere long-wave radiation: accumulated seasonal TTR is de-accumulated, sign-reversed and converted from J m⁻² to upward-positive W m⁻². VP200/CHI200 is calculated globally from 200-hPa zonal and meridional winds with windspharm, followed by removal of the weighted global mean. Coordinates are standardized, sub-daily forecasts are averaged to daily values, missing values are interpolated, and a decaying boundary adjustment reduces the analysis–forecast discontinuity.

Tropical-wave separation. The combined historical and forecast anomalies are detrended, Tukey-tapered and processed with NCICS-style longitude–time Wheeler–Kiladis Fourier filters. The displayed components are the MJO, equatorial Rossby wave, convectively coupled Kelvin wave, mixed Rossby–gravity wave and the low-frequency background state. Reflection padding is used to reduce attenuation near the forecast endpoint.

Products. Latitude-weighted fields are shown as smoothed Hovmöller diagrams and global maps for the ECMWF control, ensemble mean and selected members. Standard maps contain ten 14-day hindcast and ten 14-day forecast panels. Supplemental products contain twenty consecutive 7-day forecast means with selectable transparent wave overlays, plus an animation of consecutive 3-day means through the available seasonal endpoint.

Forecast products are updated monthly.
